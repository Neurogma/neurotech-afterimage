## Symbionic quotation check

The wording was verified in the subtitle of the 2020-12-27 Medium post: “Reflections and admittance of failure.” The article's title is “Symbionic Postmortem,” and its author is identified as Matthijs Cox. In the article Cox describes the Symbionic Project's original aim and says it did not achieve that aim. This evidence supports a firsthand account about that project only. It does not support labeling the separate active prosthetics business as failed. [S-SC-01] [S-SC-02]

## Exclusions and boundaries carried forward

- **Kernel Flow:** retained as excluded. The previously documented sources describe a research platform, not a discrete consumer product shown to have failed or been abandoned. The evidence standard has not changed. [S-SC-03]
- **Symbionic:** retained as excluded from an organizational failure dossier because the similarly named firsthand account concerns a separate project and no link to the current prosthetics company was established. [S-SC-01] [S-SC-02]
- **Victor Pikov’s subscriber whitepaper:** retained as not accessed and not reconstructed. The publisher's description is not enough to reproduce the report’s classifications or figures. [S-SC-04] [S-TR-09]
- **Freehand:** previous deferral is superseded by admission as a narrow availability/support case, not as evidence of technical failure. The original screening sources remain in the register, and the new dossier adds clinical/specialist context. [S-SC-05] [S-SC-06]
- **Trials already synthesized in the first release:** the two depression DBS and two heart-failure VNS trials are not duplicated as individual new dossiers because the existing selected review already covers them. New trial dossiers broaden indication and intervention coverage; they are not presented as a representative sample of neurostimulation research. [S-TR-01]–[S-TR-08]

The review does not establish an exhaustive candidate universe. Admission volume was subordinated to distinct, source-supported lessons. No candidate was admitted solely to hit the numeric target.

The screening logic follows the repository's distinction between product discontinuation, study termination, corporate failure and patient abandonment. In particular, an asset sale, sponsor change or terminated trial is not enough on its own to establish that patients were abandoned.
