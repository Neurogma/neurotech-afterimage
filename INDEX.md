# Index

This evidence-led collection classifies documented transitions and trial outcomes precisely. A case can be useful without being a failure. The inventory has 26 individual case dossiers, one selected clinical-trial synthesis, and one separately marked ANTHEM-HFrEF extension that is not counted as an independent case; it is not a census of neurotechnology projects.

## How to read these cases

Outcome labels describe the evidence found, not a score. “Did not meet a primary endpoint” is a trial result, not proof a whole treatment class is ineffective. “Acquired” does not mean “failed.” “Support gap reported” does not show that every user lost all support or that a device stopped working. Registry absence of results means results are not available in that registry record, not necessarily that no publication exists.
