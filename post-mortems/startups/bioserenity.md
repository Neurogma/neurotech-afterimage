## Case identity and outcome

BioSerenity developed connected diagnostic systems and services across neurology, sleep and related clinical monitoring. The outcome addressed here is the judicial liquidation of the specifically identified French legal entities BIOSERENITY SAS (SIREN 799 995 782) and BioSerenity France SAS (SIREN 817 914 278) following a court-approved transfer plan in November 2023. It is **not** a finding that the BioSerenity brand, every product, or all related operations ceased.

**Documented fact, based on company-register aggregators reproducing legal notices:** the records describe judicial reorganization proceedings opened in August 2023 and a transfer plan approved on 10 November 2023, followed by liquidation of the original entities. The commercial-information pages are not the full court file; these details should be corroborated against official BODACC/court documents before any external publication relying on legal nuance. [S-BS-01] [S-BS-02]

## What the public record shows

The public record combines registry-derived information for two distinct French legal entities with corporate announcements made under the BioSerenity name. Company-information records report proceedings and a transfer/liquidation process affecting BIOSERENITY SAS and BioSerenity France SAS; a November 2023 company release separately announced a €24 million investment by Jolt Capital and a new CEO. Because the full court file and complete asset/contract schedules were not inspected, these materials do not establish that every product line, customer contract or support obligation transferred unchanged. [S-BS-01] [S-BS-02] [S-BS-03] [S-BS-04]

### What was built

The group described connected outpatient diagnostic equipment and services used in areas including sleep, cardiology and neurology. Those systems connect technology, clinicians and data workflows, so organizational change can matter for device servicing, data access and customer relationships even when product assets are sold. The public materials reviewed do not identify the status of every installed unit or service contract. [S-BS-01] [S-BS-04]

### Chronology

- **3 August 2023 — court proceedings opened.** Company-information services reproducing commercial-register notices list judicial reorganization proceedings for the named entities. The record distinguishes each French legal entity by its SIREN identifier; that distinction matters because group names and trading brands may outlive one company. [S-BS-01] [S-BS-02]
- **10 November 2023 — transfer plan and liquidation.** The same records report a court-approved sale/transfer plan in favour of SAS Jolt Capital and liquidation of the original entities. The material reviewed here does not include a full inventory of transferred assets, contracts, employees, liabilities or product-support obligations. [S-BS-01] [S-BS-02]
- **November 2023 — successor investment announcement.** BioSerenity’s own press release said Jolt Capital invested €24 million and announced a new chief executive. The announcement establishes what the successor/business publicly said about the transaction; it does not independently prove that every former service or obligation transferred intact. [S-BS-03]
- **Later product activity.** The BioSerenity website subsequently announced regulatory/product news, including an announcement about Neuronaute Plus. This is evidence of continued activity under the brand after liquidation of the identified entities, but by itself does not resolve the corporate chain for any particular product, device or customer contract. [S-BS-04]

## What was assumed

No internal transaction memorandum or complete court file was reviewed, so the case does not attribute assumptions to the former or successor organizations. **Editorial inference:** a recurring external assumption would be that a surviving brand or a successor investment implies that all service duties continue. The sources do not support that inference. Product/brand continuity, legal-entity continuity and contract continuity must be proved separately.

## What the evidence suggests future teams should do differently

**Editorial inference:** this case is useful because “the company went into liquidation” and “the product continued” can both be true at the same time when legal entities, assets and business operations separate. Neither an investment announcement nor continuity of branding proves transfer of patient-data duties, warranty coverage, service-level commitments, cybersecurity maintenance or clinical escalation responsibility.

Teams working with connected diagnostic equipment should maintain an asset-and-obligation register that identifies the contracting entity, device manufacturer, data controller/processor, clinical service provider, software maintainer, regulator-facing legal manufacturer and funded post-transaction support owner. Transaction documents should enumerate transferred contracts and liabilities, not rely on brand continuity as a proxy.

## Consequences

The court-register-derived record demonstrates an insolvency/restructuring process affecting two identifiable entities; the press release shows investment and continuing business activity under the BioSerenity name. The cited record does not establish a broad patient-support failure, device defect, service outage, or transfer of all clinical support duties. This dossier therefore describes an organizational transition, not a clinical or technical product failure.

## What remains unknown

- The exact scope of the court-approved asset and business transfer for each product line.
- Which customer, warranty, data-protection, service and maintenance contracts were assumed, novated or terminated.
- Whether all former employees, clinical partnerships and technical records transferred.
- The current legal entity responsible for each legacy product and contract.
- The full original court orders and their schedules were not directly inspected in this review.

## Questions future teams should ask

- Which legal entity is the manufacturer and which is the service provider for each installed device?
- Are service agreements and data obligations explicitly transferred and acknowledged by the successor?
- Is a successor’s press announcement being mistaken for proof that every customer relationship continued?
- Where can customers verify replacement parts, firmware, data export and clinical escalation after a transaction?
