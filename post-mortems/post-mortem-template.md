# Documented case dossier template

This template is a guide. Omit sections that do not fit, but keep material claims sourced and distinguish fact from inference. Never use first person unless the eligible firsthand contributor authored or explicitly authorized the account.

Recommended front matter:

    ---
    case_id: unique-short-id
    title: Case title
    format: documented-case-dossier
    outcome: precise-outcome-label
    ---

## Case identity and outcome

Name the company, project, device, therapy or trial. State its purpose, relevant dates, current status and the limits of the outcome label.

## What was built

What was intended, for whom, and what was actually delivered, implanted or tested?

## What the public record shows

Reconstruct the key events in a chronology or equivalent narrative. Distinguish company statements, official records, trial results and attributed testimony.

## What was assumed

Attribute assumptions to the project team only when a source supports that. Otherwise label them as hypotheses proposed by the case analysis.

## Consequences

Describe the dimensions relevant to the case: technical performance, clinical outcome, patient burden, maintenance, evidence generation, commercialization and long-term dependencies. Quantify only when the source and meaning of the figure are clear.

## What remains unknown

List only questions that materially affect the interpretation.

## What the evidence suggests future teams should do differently

Use this heading for an editorial dossier. State the inference's limit and any competing explanation that remains plausible.

For a genuine first-person account by an eligible contributor, “What I would do differently” may be used in that contributor's own words.

## Questions future teams should ask

Write a short set of case-specific questions that can change an engineering, clinical, commercial or support decision.
